# Community

Nanolist is maintained by the Nano Collective and shaped by the people who use it. There are two easy ways to help: [submit a tool](https://list.nanocollective.org/submit) you think belongs in the directory, or pick up an [open issue](https://github.com/Nano-Collective/nanolist/issues).

## Channels

- **Discord**: [discord.gg/ktPDV6rekE](https://discord.gg/ktPDV6rekE) — the collective's main channel; say hello if you are unsure where to start.
- **GitHub issues**: [bug reports and feature requests](https://github.com/Nano-Collective/nanolist/issues).
- **Contributing**: [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/Nano-Collective/nanolist/blob/master/CONTRIBUTING.md) covers development setup, standards, and the review process.

## Code of Conduct

Nanolist follows the collective [Code of Conduct](/collective/organisation/community). We do not redefine it here.

## Donations and sponsorship

Supporting the collective's work is covered once, on the [Support page](/collective/organisation/support). Paid, scoped contribution is covered by the [Economics Charter](/collective/organisation/economics-charter).
